# 📜 Loreforge

### ▶ Open the app: **https://luxswilli4m.github.io/loreforge**

A free, private worldbuilding and campaign manager for game masters and storytellers. It runs entirely in your browser: no account, no server, no subscription.

It combines a **world wiki** (entries, maps, relationship graphs, a shareable player wiki) with **GM campaign tools** (arcs, sessions with beats, a custom calendar timeline and journals).

## Features

**World Bible.** Entries for characters, locations, factions, items, creatures and lore, each colour-coded. Every entry has a summary, custom detail fields, tags, an image, a long description and a 🔒 GM-secrets section.

**@links everywhere.** Type `@` in any text box to link an entry, or create one on the spot. Entries automatically show what they link to and everywhere they're mentioned.

**Interactive maps.** Upload any map image, then pan and zoom. Drop pins linked to entries, nest maps (a pin on the world map can open a city map, which opens a dungeon map), calibrate the scale against the map's scale bar, and measure routes with travel times on foot, horseback, wagon and ship.

**GM Screen.** A dice roller that understands rolls like `2d6+3`, `4d6kh3`, `adv` and `dis`, and highlights natural 20s and 1s. A summary of the current fight (with a link to the full combat tracker), a party sheet with levels, quick generators (NPC, tavern, loot, weather, plot hook, names) that save to the World Bible in one click, and your own weighted random tables. The 🎲 button in the corner opens a floating dice tray anywhere, and **Dice** in the sidebar has the dice table built into a page with quick rolls, saved rolls and the full roll history.

**🛡️ Character sheets.** A character or creature's World Bible page shows the story and links on the left and its character sheet on the right; the sheet fits whatever width it has. In the middle is a gear screen like an action RPG: a big portrait with equipment slots down both sides (head, cloak, armour, hands, belt and feet on the left; neck, two rings, main hand, off hand and ranged on the right) and three trinket slots under the portrait for charms, potions and keys. A two-handed weapon in the main hand crosses out the off hand (whatever was there goes back in the bag); a versatile weapon held alone uses its bigger die; off-hand weapons follow the two-weapon fighting rule for damage and a compact bag grid underneath. The portrait can be your own upload, the World Bible picture, or one of 19 preset art cards (all 13 classes, plus dragon, undead, beast, fiend, aberration and elemental); it picks the card for the class you type, like "Rogue 5", until you choose. Drag items between the bag and the slots, or double-click to equip. Items have a kind (a searchable list), rarity (coloured like loot), an icon that matches the kind unless you pick another from the icon grid (or upload a picture), weight, value in copper, silver, electrum, gold or platinum, damage built from dice like the dice table (click a die to add it, right-click to remove one) with a searchable damage type, magic bonus, all the 5e weapon properties (ammunition, finesse, heavy, light, loading, ranged, reach, thrown, two-handed, special, versatile with its two-handed die, and range) and an optional 2024 weapon mastery, AC, AC bonus or penalty, attunement and a link to a World Bible item, with a hover card. Right-click an item to equip or unequip it, compare it with any other item of the same kind (yours or another character's: average damage, AC, weight, value and more, with better and worse marked), edit, duplicate or delete it. Armour class is a base (worn armour, 10 + Dex, or your own number) plus shields and item bonuses or penalties; the net change shows next to the AC, and hovering shows the full breakdown. Initiative works the same way: Dexterity plus item bonuses and your own extra bonus (like Alert), with the breakdown on hover. **Proficiencies** are tick boxes (simple and martial weapons, light, medium and heavy armour, shields), weapons have a simple or martial category, and any item can be set to always or never proficient. By the rules as written, a weapon you're not proficient with loses your proficiency bonus to hit, and armour or a shield you're not proficient with gives disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity rolls and attacks and stops spellcasting (shown as a warning, with ADV/DIS on the affected rolls). **⚙ Rules** lets each world change all of that as house rules: proficiency bonus on or off, extra to hit and damage, advantage or disadvantage, AC changes and spellcasting, for proficient and non-proficient gear. All the 5e stats are there and editable on the sheet: ability scores and modifiers, saves, all 18 skills (proficiency or expertise), passive scores, proficiency bonus, AC (worked out from armour, shield and AC bonuses unless you set it), initiative, speed, hit points with temp HP and rollable hit dice, hit dice, death saves, inspiration, exhaustion and conditions, attacks (equipped weapons become attacks automatically, plus your own), spellcasting with save DC, attack bonus, slots for levels 1–9 and a spell list, features and traits, proficiencies, languages, senses, defences, personality, coins and carrying weight. Creatures get size, type, CR (with XP) and legendary actions. Click any modifier, save or attack to roll it. There are six sheet themes (Parchment, Hellforge, Elven grove, Dwarven hall, Arcane, Frostbound) and your own accent colour.

**🧙 Character builder.** On any character's sheet, the builder walks through class (all 13, or your own), level, species, background, ability scores (standard array, point buy, rolling 4d6 or typing them in, with species bonuses or your own +2/+1), and skills. It then fills in the sheet: scores, saves, skills, weapon and armour proficiencies, hit points and hit dice, speed, senses, languages, spell slots for the level (full, half, third and pact casters), class features up to that level, the class art card, and optional starting equipment already worn. You can also **make your own class** (hit die, saves, proficiencies, skill choices, spellcasting type and ability, unarmoured defence, features by level and a card), saved to the world and offered alongside the standard ones. Nothing is locked afterwards; every character can still use spellcasting and every field.

**Attacks** list your equipped weapons and your own custom attacks (built from an ability, proficiency, damage dice and type). **⚔ Attack** rolls to hit and damage together, doubling the dice on a critical, and shows the result under the table.

**🗺️ Battle map.** The fight from the combat tracker, on a map: any of your maps (uploaded or generated; maps with a scale in feet or metres size the grid themselves), a blank grid, or one of the ready-made top-down encounter maps. Every square is 5 ft. Generated city maps are drawn again sharply for whatever part is on screen, so zooming in doesn't pixelate them. Everyone in the fight gets a token (their portrait, coloured by side, with a health bar and a gold ring on whoever's turn it is; dropped creatures are crossed out), placed on open ground automatically. Pick up a token and the squares it can still reach light up from its speed, conditions, exhaustion, any Dash and how far it has already moved this turn; dragging further stops it at the edge, and water, buildings and walls block it. Hold Shift to move freely. Action, bonus action and reaction are tracked here too and refresh each turn, with Undo move and a right-click menu (use actions, Dash with the action or bonus action, hide from players). It also has a measuring ruler, a choice of diagonal rule (real distance, every square 5 ft, or 5–10–5), adjustable grid size and offset, zoom and pan, and Next turn. **🖥️ Player view** opens a window that mirrors the map for the table, without hidden creatures and with only allies' health.

**🖌️ Encounter map editor.** Paint top-down battle maps the way you would in Inkarnate, all drawn by code in one painterly style (no outside art). Paint ground with a brush, a box or a flood fill: water, deep water, mud, sand, dirt, grass, moss, snow, cave floor, lava, solid rock, cobblestones, flagstones, wooden floors, ship decks, carpet and masonry. Natural ground blends into its neighbours with soft, ragged edges, shallows and foam where it meets water; floors keep sharp edges, and grass and dirt creep over them. Draw walls (stone, brick, timber, palisade, ship rail, fence, hedge) and shaped floors of any outline (a deck over water, a round tower). Stamp more than fifty assets (trees, bushes, rocks, logs, reeds, tables, beds, bookshelves, a bar, fireplaces, barrels, crates, carts, tents, campfires, wells, pillars, statues, altars, ritual circles, cannons, masts, rowboats and more), turning them with A/D or Shift+scroll and resizing with W/S or Alt+scroll. Place lights; campfires, torches, braziers and crystals glow on their own. Then set the darkness, a colour mood (golden hour, dusk, moonlight, cold, old parchment, eerie, infernal) and fog. Layers can be hidden, everything can be selected, moved, duplicated and undone, and work in progress is kept as a draft. **Save to Maps** keeps it with your world; **Fight here** opens it in the battle map, where walls, solid rock, deep water and large props block movement. Eight ready-made maps to start from or fight on: a tavern, a bandit camp in the woods, crystal caverns, a ship at sea, harbour docks, a crossroads shrine, a sunken crypt and overgrown temple ruins.

**🖼️ Scene art.** The three isometric town scenes (a walled market town, a harbour town, a mining town cut into a mountainside) painted up as framed illustrations at dawn, midday, dusk and night: each floats as a diorama over distant hills, under a sky with clouds, sun or moon and stars, with windows and lamps glowing after dark, a gilt frame and a title plate. Download one, or use it as the picture for any entry.

**🐲 Bestiary.** 334 D&D 5e creatures (SRD 5.1), 492 Pathfinder 2e creatures (Monster Core) and 15 Mythos horrors (Lovecraft's public-domain creatures with original 5e stats), all as full, rollable stat blocks. Search, filter by type and CR or level, add any number straight to the combat tracker, or copy one into the World Bible with a filled-in sheet. **＋ Make a creature** builds a homebrew monster with typical numbers for its challenge rating (worked out from the official monsters), ready to tweak. Credits and licences are shown on the page and in `data/bestiary/CREDITS.md`.

**🧙 Character builder page** in the sidebar lists every character (build or rebuild) and your homebrew classes.

**Story Forge NPCs come with stats:** a fitting SRD NPC stat block (guard, veteran, spy, mage, priest, knight…) is picked from the character's story and the party's level. You can change it before saving, and the saved character gets a full sheet ready for combat.

**⚔️ Combat tracker.** It combines the best bits of trackers like DM Tools, D&D Battle Tracker and Improved Initiative. You can search the World Bible to add a character or creature, and their HP, AC, initiative bonus, CR or level, attacks and stat block fill in from their sheet; anyone else you type in by hand (HP can be dice, like `2d8+2`, and several at once are numbered). Initiative can be rolled per creature or all at once, ties go to the higher bonus, and you can drag rows to reorder. Every row shows the turn's **Action, Bonus action, Reaction and Movement** as green pills that turn red when clicked and refresh at the start of that creature's turn. **🏃 Dash** spends the action (right-click: the bonus action, for Cunning Action or Step of the Wind) and adds its speed again. Speed follows conditions (grappled, restrained… are 0; hasted doubles) and **exhaustion** (2014 rules by default, or the 2024 rules in Options), and exhaustion syncs with character sheets. The search also finds every bestiary creature, and library creatures show their full stat block in the details panel. Next and previous turn work with `]` and `[`, and you can skip defeated enemies. It tracks the round, in-game time and real time. In the damage box, `7` is damage, `+7` heals and `t5` gives temporary HP; temp HP is used up first. Allies who drop to 0 fall unconscious and get death-save pips, with massive damage killing outright. There are concentration reminders (with the save DC), conditions with descriptions and countdowns, reaction and legendary-action pips, lair actions on initiative 20, group damage and healing (including half damage for a save), hidden creatures and a combat log. The difficulty meter (Easy to Deadly) uses CR and party levels. You can save encounters to run later or turn someone into a World Bible entry with a sheet. Characters' hit points are written back to their sheets. **🖥️ Player view** opens a window for your players showing the turn order, who's up next and how everyone looks (Healthy, Hurt, Bloodied, Defeated), without monster numbers.

**📏 Units.** World settings has Metric (the default) or Imperial. Character sheets, the combat tracker and new maps' scale follow it (5 ft = 1.5 m, 1 lb = 0.5 kg).

**☁️ Cloud & devices.** You can optionally run your own free server on Cloudflare (see [cloudflare/SETUP.md](cloudflare/SETUP.md)). With it you have one login across all your devices, and worlds, maps and Library files sync automatically. Every device keeps an offline copy. If a world was edited on two devices before they synced, Loreforge asks which version to keep (or keeps both). The server also runs Story Forge's AI using OpenRouter's free models, with Cloudflare Workers AI as a backup, so no keys are stored on your devices.

**🔮 Story Forge.** Ask for a bounty, quest, rumours, NPC, encounter or anything else. Choose where it's set and which factions, religions or lore it should tie into, plus the tone and party level. It reads your world (the location's ruler, its districts, the locals and the factions present) and builds the story around them. Bounties come with a Wanted poster. One click saves the result as a quest, creates the target and quest giver as entries with relationships, and @links everything; you can also save the poster as a player handout. There are two engines:
- *Built-in (free, offline):* a story engine that combines your world's own people, places and faiths with the keywords in your request.
- *AI (optional):* your own Cloudflare server, OpenRouter (free models available), Anthropic Claude, any OpenAI-compatible service, or a local model through Ollama. Your key is stored only in your browser and never included in backups. Requests (with the related parts of your world) go straight from your browser to the provider and are billed to your account.

**Quests.** A drag-and-drop board (Rumoured / Active / Completed / Failed). Each quest has objectives, a quest giver, a reward and details, with @links throughout.

**Library.** Drop in PDF rulebooks, adventures, handouts and images, then read them in the app or Ctrl+click to open them in the side panel next to your notes.

**Undo and milestones.** Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo anything you've done since opening the app, with fast typing grouped into one step, like Word. Inside a text box, Ctrl+Z works on the text as usual. ⭐ Milestone saves a named, permanent backup whenever you choose, to your GitHub repo, your server, or this device. Restore one from World settings; your current version is kept as a milestone first. Full backups (.json) include pictures and Library files, and you get a reminder when you haven't backed up in two weeks.

**Lightweight worlds.** Pictures and maps are stored as separate files in a compact format and referenced by the world, so worlds stay small and sync quickly. Older worlds convert automatically.

**Little things.** ⭐ Pin favourite entries to the sidebar, 🎲 get name suggestions when creating entries, and press **?** for keyboard shortcuts.

**Map generator.** Create fantasy maps from a seed, in Parchment, Atlas or Night style.
- *World maps:* continents, archipelagos or coastal regions, with mountain ranges, forests, rivers, lakes, biomes, realms and their borders, towns, capitals, roads, and named seas, mountains and forests.
- *City maps:* villages up to capital cities, with walls and gates, districts, building lots, a market square, castle, temple, gardens, a river with bridges, and harbour docks.
- Saving a map can drop pins on every town or landmark and create World Bible entries for towns (Locations) and realms (Factions), linked by relationships. From any pin, "Generate a city map" makes a city that opens from that pin.

**World Web.** A live web of your world built from named, directional connections ("rules", "ally of"…) and @mentions. Right-click a node to connect it to another node (click the target or search for it), create a new connected entry, add a dated note, focus on it, pin it or delete it. Right-click a connection to rename, reverse or delete it, and right-click empty space to add an entry there. Shift+drag from node to node also connects them. Nodes you arrange stay where you put them.

The web has **the Cosmos** at its centre (solar systems and other cosmos entries hang straight off it) and a **master node** for each entry type, with every entry hanging off its type. **Places inside places** nest as sub-nodes: towns inside their realm, realms inside their planet. Set this with an entry's **Part of** field, a map's **This map shows** setting, or by attaching a map to a planet in the Cosmos. Generated world maps do it automatically, and saving an edited map adds new realms and towns to the World Bible in the right place. Relationships are **coloured by kind** (hostile red, love pink, family amber, friendly green, power purple, place blue). The kind is worked out from the wording, or you can pick it. Filter to one kind, or choose *None* to see just the tree. Fold any node to tuck its sub-nodes away, or use the **Depth** slider to choose how many levels of sub-nodes show. Nodes float freely and settle around anything you drag. Middle-drag pans. Double-click a place to open its map. **🌳 Family tree** lays characters out by generation from *parent of*, *child of*, *married to* and *sibling of* relationships, and can show one person's family.

New entry type: **🪐 Cosmos** for planets, stars and planes. The Cosmos editor creates entries of this type. The Cosmos star chart can hold several **solar systems** (＋ Solar system): each has its own star and its own World Bible entry, with planets nested inside it in the World Web. The **🌌 Universe** view shows every system at once, and you can double-click a world to fly into its system. Pick a system from the searchable, alphabetical list in the header. Middle-drag pans the view. Right-click a world to edit it, add a moon or an orbiting world, connect it, add it to the World Bible, open its maps, or move it to another system.

**Encyclopedia.** Every entry, journal, session, map and timeline event in one index. Sort it A–Z (with a letter bar), by category or by tag, filter it, and search it. Articles open in a reading pane, and links inside an article open within the Encyclopedia.

**Appearance.** Five vibes (Tavern, Parchment, Arcane, Verdant, Ember) and torn-paper panels with adjustable tear depth and opacity. By default the background is a freshly generated fantasy map; you can also choose a preset, one of your own maps, or an uploaded image, then darken or soften it.

**Campaigns and sessions.** Group sessions into arcs. Plan each session as a checklist of beats you tick off at the table, with prep notes, a recap, and an auto-generated list of the NPCs and places featured.

**Timeline and custom calendar.** Set your own months, day counts, weekdays and eras. World events and dated sessions appear on one timeline, with a month calendar view.

**Journals.** Long-form writing for session logs, history, handouts and house rules.

**Side panel.** Ctrl+click (⌘+click on Mac) any link to open it beside what you're working on.

**Search.** Ctrl+K searches everything.

**Player wiki export.** One self-contained HTML file with entries, maps, timeline and history/handout journals. GM secrets and session prep are left out.

**Backups.** Export and import your whole world as a `.json` file, which is also how you move it to another computer.

**Accessibility.** Adjustable text size and an easy-read font (Atkinson Hyperlegible).

## Using it

Open the GitHub Pages link for this repository, then choose **Try a sample world** to see everything working together, or **New world** to start fresh.

> ⚠️ Your worlds are stored in your browser (IndexedDB). Clearing site data deletes them, so use **World settings → Export backup** regularly.

## Running locally

It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so there's no build step:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/luxswilli4m/loreforge.git
cd loreforge
python3 -m http.server 8000   # then open http://localhost:8000
```

(It must be served over http rather than opened as a file, because it uses ES modules.)

### Map editor & styles
World maps can be edited by hand after generating (✏️ **Edit this map**): terrain brushes (raise, lower, mountains, smooth, flatten, add land, add sea), biome and realm painting, and adding, moving, renaming and deleting towns and capitals. Rivers, lakes, forests, borders and roads recalculate after every stroke, with undo/redo. Saved world maps keep their editable terrain, so **✏️ Edit terrain, towns & style** on a map reopens it in the editor.

Eight world styles: Parchment, Atlas, Night, **Ink & Quill** (pen-and-ink epic fantasy), **Storybook** (bright and colourful), **Painted** (painterly with cloud edges), **Realistic** (shaded elevation) and **Political** (coloured realm cells).

City maps have five styles, including **Storybook** (coloured roofs, cobbled streets, lush trees, painted water) and **Ink & Quill**.

### Drawing on maps
Any map, generated or uploaded, has a 🖌️ **Draw** mode: pen, marker, line, box, circle, text (serif, calligraphy or plain), 18 map stamps (castle, tower, village, temple, ruins, camp, cave, peak, grove, ship, port, sea beast, danger, battle, X marks the spot, flag, magic, star), an eraser, a move tool and layers you can show or hide. Hidden layers are left out of the player wiki, so they're handy for GM-only notes. Hold Space or use the middle mouse button to pan while drawing.

The generator fills the screen: scroll to zoom, drag to move (Space, middle or right mouse button while editing). City maps can show more countryside (**Area shown**: close up → whole valley) and can be edited by hand: add, remove and repaint buildings, plant or clear trees, add houses in tight street-aligned rows (Uniform) or scattered (Loose), draw as many walls as you like (closed loops or open lines; gates open where roads cross) and knock them down, draw roads that latch onto existing roads (Connected) or go anywhere (Free), colour and remove roads, and draw rivers and lakes or drain them. Right-click cancels a wall, road, river or lake you're drawing. River width is a slider. **🏛️ Buildings & sets** places 20 fantasy buildings (castle, church, cathedral, chapel, blacksmith, tavern, inn, guild hall, town hall, mage tower, watchtower, barracks, stables, windmill, granary, manor house, library, alchemist, bathhouse, arena). It also places 7 sets with their own streets (market square, town block, temple square, castle compound, crossroads hamlet, noble estate, farmstead). Rotate them with the scroll wheel or A / D, resize them, let their streets join onto nearby roads, and drag a placed piece to move it. Rivers and roads are drawn the same way in the world and city editors: **Freehand** (drag) or **Point to point** (click, then double-click or Enter). **Edit & bend** (world and city) lets you pick up any road or river (generated rivers too) to change its width with a slider (mouth and source width for drawn world rivers) and pull it into a curve. Double-click the line to add an elbow where it should articulate, and double-click an elbow to remove it. **Map icons** (castles, cities and crests, mines, barrows, dwarven ruins, forts, shrines, dragon lairs, giant camps, strongholds, ships, portals and more, 47 in all, in a style that matches the map or as flat cream **Marker** icons) can be dropped onto both city and world maps, then dragged, resized, flipped or removed; they redraw to match the map style.

**Map size:** world maps come in km presets (Region 400 × 250 up to Whole world 16,000 × 8,000) or any custom size; cities from Close up (0.8 × 0.64 km) to Whole valley (6 × 4.5 km) or custom. Zooming in redraws the visible part at full sharpness. In the generator the frame, title, compass and scale bar stay pinned to the edges of the view (the scale bar follows the zoom), and **📸 Snapshot** saves exactly what's inside the frame as a picture attached to the map.

**Map folders:** every map card has a 📁 folder showing the map and its snapshots. Snapshots have names and captions with @ links, so they appear on the entries they mention.

**More generator tools:** a landmass template picker (Continent, Large continent, Twin continents, Old world, Pangea, High/Low island, Volcano, Atoll, Archipelago, Shattered, Fractious, Mediterranean, Peninsula, Isthmus, Coastal region, Desert continent) with live previews, plus **Import heightmap** from any greyscale picture. A **Pastel political** style (Azgaar-like), a **Layers** panel (realm colours, borders, mountains, forests, rivers, roads, towns and each kind of label), hand-drawn rivers and roads, a dry-up-rivers brush, custom and renamed labels, and Regenerate buttons for names, borders, towns and automatic roads. **Whole world** maps wrap east–west with polar ice and can be viewed as a spinning **🌐 globe**.

**Timeline overview:** switch the Timeline to ⟷ Overview for a big horizontal chart with era bands, events above the line and sessions below. Scroll to zoom from millennia down to days, drag to move, hover anywhere to read the date and hover an event for its details. The calendar zooms out (click its title) from month → year (all months) → twelve-year view.

**🌌 Cosmos:** a spinnable 3D star chart of worlds, moons, stars, planes and demiplanes with animated portals, routes, orbits and bonds. Hover for names, click for details and the maps attached to each world, double-click to fly to it, Shift-drag to reposition it. In the editor, search to attach maps, link or create a World Bible entry, and pick a Type (or name your own custom type).



## 🛠️ Asset Forge
Make your own assets. (Turn a selected shape with the mouse wheel or A / D.) You can draw **custom map icons** from boxes, ovals, click-point shapes, lines and freehand strokes in the same 100×100 box as the built-in icons. Fills use colour *roles* (Stone, Roof, Leaf, Gold…), so each icon repaints to match every map style; an exact colour stays fixed. The **🔷 Shapes** gallery has 29 ready-made shapes (stars, hearts, arrows, shields, banners, crescents, clouds, battlements and more): drag one onto the box or click it to drop it in the middle. With Select & move, drag a corner handle to resize (Shift keeps the proportions) or the round handle on top to turn a shape. **Snap to grid** locks points, corners, moves and resizes to every half grid square, and turns in 15° steps; freehand is never snapped, and holding Alt ignores the grid for a moment. You can add a picture to trace over, and preview the icon in Parchment, Storybook, Night, Ink & Quill and Marker looks. Custom icons appear in the map generator's 🐉 Map icons palette under the group name you give them. The Asset Forge is built so more kinds of custom asset can be added later.

**Right-click menus:** in the map viewer (select or deselect pins, open, rename, measure, add a pin, drawing tools) and in the map generator (select or deselect icons and lines, quick tools, undo, fit). Right-drag still pans in the generator.


## 🎲 Dice table
From the dice tray (or **Dice** in the sidebar, where it's built into the page), **Open the dice table** opens a big window where dice really roll. Build a pool (click a die to add one, right-click to remove one, set a modifier) and hit Roll. The dice are real 3D shapes (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20): they tumble through the air, roll across the tray through their faces, bounce off the walls and each other, then tip over onto the face that was rolled. A d100 is rolled as two percentile dice, tens and units; the total and every die are shown alongside. Natural 20s glow gold and natural 1s red, and dice dropped by advantage or disadvantage are faded. Click the tray to roll again. While the table is open, rolls from the tray or the GM screen tumble there too. There are five trays (Tavern table, Arcane velvet, Dungeon stone, Explorer's map, Starfall), or you can use **your own picture**. **Dice materials:** Match the tray, Ivory, Oak wood, Steel, Brass, Gemstone, Marble, Obsidian and Bone, each with its own texture and shine. You can also choose **your own picture**: one image becomes the surface of every face of every die. You can change the dice colour and the number colour on any of them. The window can be dragged anywhere and remembers your settings.
